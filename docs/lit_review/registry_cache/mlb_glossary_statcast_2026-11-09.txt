Title: Statcast | Glossary | MLB.com

Statcast was installed in all 30 MLB ballparks in 2015 after a partial trial run in 2014.

From 2015-19, Statcast consisted of a combination of camera and radar systems. That changed in 2020, with the arrival of Hawk-Eye.
